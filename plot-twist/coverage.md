Source scope: the eleven uploaded Skew the Script 1.A.1–1.A.6 and 1.B.1–1.B.5 lesson keys, checked against the uploaded College Board Unit 1 framework (topics 1.1–1.13). Original scenarios and questions; not reproductions of the handouts.

AP safeguards: quantitative units and context; SRS subset criterion; sampling versus assignment; conditional bias direction; no z-to-percentile claim without a distribution; both supplied outlier rules; sample SD n−1; percentile at-or-below convention. Quartile algorithm stated where needed. Short answer selection practices interpretation but does not replace written AP justification: ask students to explain a missed ladder aloud or in writing after play.

Scoring: 10 per correct decision, no time or streak bonus, no penalty for wrong answers, every rung remains available. Total points still reward completing more questions within the class timer; untimed practice removes that pressure. Correct answer locations are shuffled reproducibly. Every student in a room receives the same case order for that round.
